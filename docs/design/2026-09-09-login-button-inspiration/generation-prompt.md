# 登录按钮灵感图生成记录

方式：内置 image_gen，以上传的现有登录背景为视觉参考生成四款静态按钮方案。仅用于比较视觉方向，不是 Figma 可编辑组件或已实现功能。

参考背景：`miniprogram/pages/login/background-unified-blue-slogan-dot.jpg`。

外部灵感参考：[Sadia Ahmed Chandni 的玻璃质感登录设计](https://dribbble.com/shots/27181540-Glassmorphism-Login-UI)、[Ana Cacija 的移动端登录设计](https://www.behance.net/gallery/158248803/Login-Registration-Mobile)。对比图中的四款为本次自行构思的方案。

## 完整提示词

```text
Use case: ui-mockup / style-transfer.
Asset type: four-option login button visual inspiration board for CampusX, a Chinese campus mini-program. This is a static concept comparison, not production UI.
Input image is the existing login background and must guide the campus setting and blue palette. Reuse / visually adapt this blue sky, glass university architecture and green tree campus artwork as the SAME quiet backdrop crop in each of four preview panels. Focus on the login BUTTONS, not redesigning the whole page.

Create a refined 1600×1400 landscape-ish design board with an off-white #F4F7FB outer background, a small editorial header "CampusX · 登录按钮灵感", and four generously sized preview cards in a strict 2×2 grid. Each card has a small white caption strip at top with its exact numbered Chinese title; below it is the same blue campus background crop with a small amount of blur/softening, where a large beautifully crafted button is centered. No device frames, no account fields, no full screen screenshots, no logos or UI clutter. All four examples should be directly comparable, polished and visually DISTINCT. Chinese lettering must be accurate and crisp. Label all 4 options clearly.

TOP LEFT title "01  白色胶囊"
An opaque warm white pill button, 300×56 logical px, generous radius 28, centered dark royal blue "登录" label with a small simple blue right arrow on the right, subtle low-opacity blue-tinted shadow and a clean sharp edge. Minimal, calm, premium. It should stand out clearly over the illustrated blue background without obscuring lots of artwork.

TOP RIGHT title "02  蓝色渐变"
A rich saturated royal blue wide pill button, 300×56 logical px, subtle left #448FFF to right #2463E8 gradient, centered white "登录" and a thin white arrow. Faint top-edge highlight, restrained soft shadow. No neon glow, no plastic 3D.

BOTTOM LEFT title "03  磨砂圆形"
A standalone circular arrow login button about 72×72 logical px, pearl-white translucent frosted glass, thin bright white rim, soft blue-tinted depth shadow, a crisp royal blue right arrow in the center. Tiny clean dark-blue text "登录" directly below the circle, outside the button. The blue background should faintly show through the circle while the arrow stays high contrast. Closest to the existing icon-only round login affordance.

BOTTOM RIGHT title "04  分段箭头"
A distinctive white pill button about 300×56 logical px, dark-blue "登录" label aligned in the left two-thirds, and a vivid royal blue filled CIRCLE about 44×44 logical px nested with 6 px inset at the right end containing a crisp WHITE right arrow. Cohesive single control with an inset circular accent, subtle shadow. The white body and blue arrow module form a clear two-part composition.

Overall art direction: clear and readable calm campus blue-and-white aesthetic, beautifully measured spacing and subtle materials, consistent scale, perfect alignment, smooth antialiasing, professional Figma-ready design exploration. No long explanatory copy, no ratings, no fake recommendation badges, no additional login providers, no WeChat/Apple/Google marks, no username/password controls, no disabled controls, no watermark. Exactly 4 style examples and exactly the Chinese captions specified. The campus background is for context; each button must remain the dominant focal point.
```
