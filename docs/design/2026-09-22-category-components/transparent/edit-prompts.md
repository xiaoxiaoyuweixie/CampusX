# 分类组件透明底编辑记录

使用内置 image_gen，以上一版五张组件 PNG 为编辑目标。仅移除浅色圆角底块之外的白色画布，保留底块及图标内部白色。原始白底文件保留。

## 数码电子

源文件：../01-electronics.png

```text
Use case: background-extraction. Input image is the edit target: the existing CampusX "数码电子" category component. Remove ONLY the pure white canvas background OUTSIDE its large pastel rounded-square tile and replace those outer pixels with TRUE alpha transparency. Keep the entire existing pastel rounded-square tile, its exact silhouette, its softly colored fill and every pixel of the blue icon and its white/light-blue interior areas unchanged and fully opaque. This is strictly a background removal task, NOT a redesign. Do not remove pale background color inside the rounded square. Do not punch holes in white parts of the phone, papers, books, hands or lamp. Do not redraw, recolor, move, resize or restyle the component. Preserve the original square canvas dimensions and original component placement; keep transparent padding around it. Trace the existing smooth rounded-square boundary accurately, using a clean antialiased alpha edge with no white halo, white border, fringe, jagged pixels, or stray specks outside the tile. The outer four corners must have alpha zero. Output a genuinely transparent RGBA PNG, not an image of a checkerboard, no replacement colored background, no grid, no shadow, no caption or watermark. Inside the rounded-square tile must remain fully opaque; transparency applies ONLY outside the tile.
```
